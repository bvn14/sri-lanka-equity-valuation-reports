## Report summaries

### John Keells Holdings PLC (JKH)
- Task: value the company, compare it with the share price on 2 May 2025, and give a recommendation.
- Intrinsic valuation: five-year DCF using EBITDA growth, a WACC estimate and a terminal growth rate.
- Relative valuation: P/E, P/BV and EV/EBITDA compared with local peers.
- Also covers segment opportunities, risks and the macroeconomic context of Sri Lanka.

### Ceylon Beverage Holdings PLC (BREW)
- Task: same as above.
- Intrinsic valuation: FCFF model with a five-year forecast and terminal value.
- Relative valuation: P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B, P/S and price to cash flow against Lion Brewery and Ceylon Cold Stores.
